# Essentials of a Valid Contract

> [!tip] Exam issue
> Explain the essentials of a valid contract with reference to the Indian Contract Act, 1872.

## Introduction and statutory foundation

The Indian Contract Act, 1872 distinguishes an agreement from an enforceable contract. **Section 2(h)** describes a contract as an agreement enforceable by law. **Section 10** identifies core requirements for an agreement to be a contract: free consent of competent parties; lawful consideration and object; and not being expressly declared void. Formal requirements under other applicable laws can still matter.

## Essential conditions (answer headings)

1. **Agreement:** a proposal accepted according to law. For formation, address sections 2, 3–9, especially communication and absolute acceptance.
2. **Competent parties:** section 11 deals with majority, sound mind and legal disqualification; section 12 concerns sound mind.
3. **Free consent:** sections 13–14 distinguish consent and free consent; discuss coercion (s.15), undue influence (s.16), fraud (s.17), misrepresentation (s.18) and mistake (ss.20–22) as the question requires.
4. **Lawful consideration and lawful object:** address section 23; where relevant, exceptions to the rule about consideration under section 25.
5. **Agreement not expressly void:** illustrations include restraint of marriage, trade, legal proceedings and uncertainty (ss.26–29), subject to statutory details and exceptions.
6. **Compliance with required formalities:** some transactions require writing, attestation or registration under other legislation; section 10 preserves such requirements.

## Illustration

A and B agree to transfer a lawful movable item for an agreed price, both being competent adults who freely consent. This may constitute a valid contract if the remaining legal conditions are met. Contrast an agreement involving an unlawful object, which fails the legal enforceability test.

## Conclusion

The essence of a contract is not agreement alone, but **legal enforceability**. In an exam answer, move from section 2(h) to section 10, and then unpack the related provisions methodically.

## Short answer (revision)

Valid contract = agreement enforceable by law (s.2(h)) satisfying s.10: competence, free consent, lawful consideration, lawful object and absence of express statutory invalidity. Check additional mandatory formalities.
