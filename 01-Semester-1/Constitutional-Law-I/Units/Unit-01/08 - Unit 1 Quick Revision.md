# Unit 1 — Two-Minute Revision

## Seven things to remember

1. **Constitution:** Higher framework for government, powers and rights.
2. **Types:** Written/uncodified, rigid/flexible, federal/unitary.
3. **Constitutionalism:** Real limits on government power.
4. **Indian features:** Parliamentary government, federal powers, rights, judiciary, Directive Principles.
5. **Preamble:** Explains constitutional identity and goals.
6. **Four values:** Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity (**JLEF**).
7. **Citizenship:** Articles **5–11**, Citizenship Act **sections 3–10**, plus special provisions.

## Key distinctions

| Remember | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| Constitution / constitutionalism | Governing framework / meaningful limits in practice |
| Preamble / operative Article | Statement of aims / specific constitutional rule |
| Renunciation / termination | Formal giving up / voluntary foreign citizenship |

## One exam-writing formula

**Define → Explain → Relevant provision → Example/case → Conclude.**

## Self-check

**Q:** Which Article gives Parliament citizenship power? **A:** Article 11.

**Q:** What does fraternity protect? **A:** Dignity, unity and integrity.
