# India’s Constitution: Key Features
*Nine features, simply explained*
## Quick idea
The Indian Constitution brings together **democracy, a federal structure, constitutional rights and independent courts**.

## Understand
1. **Written and detailed:** A comprehensive legal document.
2. **Constitutional supremacy:** Government action must comply with it.
3. **Parliamentary system:** The Union Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha.
4. **Federal allocation:** Powers are allocated between the Union and the States.
5. **Fundamental Rights:** Part III protects rights.
6. **Directive Principles:** Part IV guides State policy and is not directly enforceable in the same way as Fundamental Rights.
7. **Independent judiciary:** Courts exercise constitutional adjudication.
8. **Secular democratic republic:** Important commitments reflected in the Preamble.
9. **Single citizenship:** Citizenship is national, rather than separate State citizenship.

## Write in the exam
Open by explaining that India's Constitution is **written, supreme and democratic**. Explain six or more features in brief paragraphs rather than only writing a list.

## Check yourself
**Q:** What is the main role of Directive Principles?

**A:** They guide government policy and lawmaking.
