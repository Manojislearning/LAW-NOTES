# Unit 1 — Practise for Exams

> [!note] Practice, not past-paper questions
> These are original questions based on the syllabus, **not** verified KSLU previous-year questions.

## Seven short questions

1. What is a constitution?
2. Name three classifications of constitutions.
3. What is constitutionalism?
4. Identify five Indian constitutional features.
5. Why is the Preamble important?
6. What are the Preamble's four main values?
7. Which Article authorises Parliament to regulate citizenship?

## Three long questions

8. Explain the difference between a constitution and constitutionalism.
9. Discuss the meaning, values and legal significance of the Preamble.
10. Explain modes of acquisition and loss of Indian citizenship with statutory sections.

## Short answer cues

1. Fundamental framework; organs, limits and rights.
2. Written/uncodified; rigid/flexible; federal/unitary.
3. Government under effective legal restrictions.
4. Parliamentary system, federalism, rights, independence of judiciary, supremacy.
5. States founding aims and guides constitutional interpretation.
6. Justice, liberty, equality, fraternity.
7. Article 11.

## Long answer outline

- **Q8:** Definition → rule of law → rights → review → limited power → conclusion.
- **Q9:** People → identity → four values → interpretive role → verified case.
- **Q10:** Articles 5–11 → sections 3–7 and 6B → sections 8–10 → current-law caution.
