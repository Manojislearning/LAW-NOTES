# What Is a Constitution?
*The rulebook of a country*
## Quick idea
A **constitution** is the fundamental legal framework of a country. It explains **who governs, what powers they have, how those powers are limited and which rights people enjoy**.

## Understand
- **Institutions:** It creates and regulates bodies such as the legislature, executive and judiciary.
- **Powers:** It allocates authority and sets limits.
- **Rights:** It protects people through legal guarantees and constitutional principles.
- **Supremacy:** In India, public institutions and ordinary laws must follow the Constitution.

> [!example] Example
> Parliament makes laws, but courts may review laws for compliance with the Constitution.

## Write in the exam
**Definition:** A constitution is the fundamental framework that establishes government, distributes and limits public power and protects constitutional rights.

**Answer order:** Meaning → institutions → powers and limits → rights → importance.

## Check yourself
**Q:** Why is a constitution more than an ordinary law?

**A:** It provides the higher framework within which ordinary laws and government institutions operate.
