# Four Values of the Preamble

*Justice · Liberty · Equality · Fraternity*

## Quick idea
The Preamble expresses **four connected aims**: justice, liberty, equality and fraternity. Remember **J–L–E–F**.

## Understand
**Justice:** Social, economic and political fairness.

**Liberty:** Freedom of thought, expression, belief, faith and worship.

**Equality:** Equal status and opportunity.

**Fraternity:** Shared belonging, dignity of every person, and the nation's unity and integrity.

India is also described as a **sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic republic**.

## What changed in 1976?
The **42nd Constitutional Amendment** added **socialist**, **secular**, and the reference to **integrity** in the Preamble. These wording changes took effect in 1977.

## Write in the exam
Introduce the Preamble, explain the four values in four short paragraphs, then connect dignity, unity and constitutional democracy.

## Check yourself
**Q:** Which ideal is linked to the dignity of the individual?

**A:** Fraternity.

## Memory cue
**J**ustice — fairness. **L**iberty — freedom. **E**quality — equal standing. **F**raternity — dignity and belonging.
