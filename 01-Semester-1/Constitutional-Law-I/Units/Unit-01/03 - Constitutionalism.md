# Constitutionalism
*Government must obey limits*
## Quick idea
A country can have a constitution without fully practising **constitutionalism**. Constitutionalism means that **government is effectively restrained by law**.

## Understand
- **Limited government:** Officials act only within legal authority.
- **Rule of law:** No individual or institution is above the law.
- **Accountability:** Government choices are subject to checks.
- **Rights and judicial review:** Courts may examine the constitutionality of state action.

**Constitution** = the governing framework.  
**Constitutionalism** = power actually being limited by that framework.

> [!example] Example
> If an official acts beyond lawful powers, legal review provides a possible check.

## Case connection
In *Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala* (1973), the Supreme Court recognised the **basic structure doctrine**, limiting Parliament's power to amend the Constitution. This does not prohibit every constitutional amendment.

## Write in the exam
Definition → limited government → rule of law → rights and accountability → brief conclusion.

## Check yourself
**Q:** What distinguishes a constitution from constitutionalism?

**A:** One provides rules; the other requires government to respect effective restraints.
