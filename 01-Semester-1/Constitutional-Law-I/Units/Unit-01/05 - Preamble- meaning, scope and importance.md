# The Preamble: Why It Matters

*The opening guide to the Constitution*

## Quick idea
The **Preamble** introduces the Constitution's **source of authority, national identity and main ideals**. It helps explain what the Constitution seeks to achieve.

## Understand
- **People's authority:** The Constitution opens by identifying the people of India as its makers.
- **India's identity:** It declares India sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic and republican.
- **Four ideals:** Justice, liberty, equality and fraternity.
- **Interpretation:** It helps courts and readers understand constitutional purposes, but is **not by itself a separate grant of legal powers**.

**Meaning:** The Constitution's opening statement.  
**Scope:** Its interpretive and guiding role.  
**Importance:** It conveys the constitutional vision.

## Case connection
*Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala* (1973) is an important case when explaining the Preamble and the basic structure doctrine.

## Write in the exam
Definition → people's authority → constitutional identity → four ideals → interpretive role → conclusion.

## Check yourself
**Q:** Can the Preamble alone give an official new legal powers?

**A:** No. Legal powers need support from operative law; the Preamble helps explain the constitutional context.
