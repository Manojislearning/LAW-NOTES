# Meaning, Kinds and Salient Features of Constitutions

> [!warning] Study status

**Unit:** [[Unit-01 - Constitution and Preamble]] · **Map:** [[08-Brain-Branches/Constitutional-Law-I/Unit-01 - Constitution and Preamble|Open Unit 1 Brain Branches]]

## Meaning of a Constitution

A constitution lays down the foundational arrangements for the exercise of public power: government institutions, their competences, procedures, limits and the rights of persons. In an exam answer, distinguish a constitution as a legal framework from the broader political practice through which institutions operate.

## Written and Unwritten

- **Codified / written:** Foundational constitutional rules are principally gathered in a formal document, such as India's Constitution.
- **Uncodified / convention-based:** Important constitutional rules are spread across legislation, judicial decisions and constitutional conventions, as in the United Kingdom.
- Avoid suggesting that an 'unwritten' constitution has no written sources.

## Rigid and Flexible

- **Rigid:** Change normally requires a special constitutional amendment process.
- **Flexible:** Some constitutional rules may be changed by ordinary legislative procedures.
- For India, discuss Article 368 and the different amendment routes rather than describing the whole document as exclusively rigid or exclusively flexible.

## Federal and Unitary

- **Federal features:** Constitutional allocation of powers between Union and States; judicial resolution of constitutional disputes.
- **Unitary features:** Features enabling coordinated or stronger Union action in particular circumstances.
- For India, support each point with the relevant constitutional article when preparing the final answer.

## Features of the Indian Constitution

Revise: a lengthy written Constitution; parliamentary government; distribution of legislative powers; fundamental rights; directive principles; independent judiciary; constitutional remedies; universal adult franchise; and amendment processes.
