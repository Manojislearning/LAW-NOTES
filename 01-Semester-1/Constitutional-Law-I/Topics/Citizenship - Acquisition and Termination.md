# Citizenship — Acquisition and Termination

> [!warning] Current-law caution
> Citizenship law depends on the date of birth and statutory amendments, rules and notifications. This outline is **not** a statement of all current eligibility conditions. Verify directly against authoritative texts before exam use.

**Unit:** [[Unit-01 - Constitution and Preamble]] · **Map:** [[08-Brain-Branches/Constitutional-Law-I/Unit-01 - Constitution and Preamble|Open Unit 1 Brain Branches]]

## Constitutional Framework

Study **Articles 5–11** of the Constitution, especially the distinction between citizenship at the Constitution's commencement and Parliament's power to regulate citizenship thereafter.

## Acquisition by Birth and Descent

The Citizenship Act, 1955 contains provisions for citizenship by birth (section 3) and descent (section 4). The applicable conditions changed over time; prepare a date-sensitive comparison table from the current Act.

## Registration Naturalisation and Territory

Read section 5 (registration), section 6 (naturalisation) and section 7 (incorporation of territory). For each, distinguish the legal route from the conditions, procedures and documentary requirements.

## Renunciation Termination and Deprivation

Study section 8 (renunciation), section 9 (termination) and section 10 (deprivation). Do **not** treat these as interchangeable; make a three-column comparison showing who initiates the process and the operative legal circumstances.
