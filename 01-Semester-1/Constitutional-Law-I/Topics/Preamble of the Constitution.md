# Preamble of the Constitution

> [!warning] Study status
> This is a revision framework. Read the Preamble verbatim from the official text, and verify judicial propositions from reported decisions before writing them in an exam.

**Unit:** [[Unit-01 - Constitution and Preamble]] · **Map:** [[08-Brain-Branches/Constitutional-Law-I/Unit-01 - Constitution and Preamble|Open Unit 1 Brain Branches]]

The Preamble states the Constitution's foundational objectives and values. It offers a concise framework for explaining constitutional identity, purposes and interpretive principles.

## Purpose and Interpretation

Explain the opening words “We, the People of India” and the Preamble's role in expressing the founding ideals. Distinguish **interpretive significance** from an independent, enforceable source of powers.

## Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic

Study the constitutional meaning of each expression separately:
- **Sovereign:** Political and legal independence.
- **Socialist:** Constitutional commitment relating to social and economic justice; interpret in context.
- **Secular:** State relationship to religion and constitutional religious freedom.
- **Democratic:** Government accountable through democratic processes.
- **Republic:** Head of State is not a hereditary monarch.

## Justice Liberty Equality Fraternity

- **Justice:** Social, economic and political.
- **Liberty:** Thought, expression, belief, faith and worship.
- **Equality:** Status and opportunity.
- **Fraternity:** Dignity of the individual and unity and integrity of the Nation.

Relate these ideals to the rights, institutions and directive principles elsewhere in the Constitution.

## Amendment and Basic Structure

Study the 42nd Constitutional Amendment's changes to the Preamble and the Supreme Court's treatment of the Preamble, constitutional amendment and the basic structure doctrine. Verify exact holdings and citations before adding cases to your answer.

## How to Write the Exam Answer

Start with the object of the Preamble, explain each key expression, analyse interpretive use, discuss amendment/basic structure with **verified judgments**, and end by linking its ideals to the constitutional scheme.
