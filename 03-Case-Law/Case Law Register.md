# Case Law Register

Each case should have **verified citation**, material facts, legal issue, ratio, exceptions and a direct relevance link to an exam question. Merely memorising a case name is not sufficient.

## Starting examples

- [[Donoghue v Stevenson - 1932]] — historic English negligence authority (not binding Indian precedent).
- Add Indian authorities for each subject after verifying the original judgment and later treatment.

## Source priority

Supreme Court official reports and e-SCR for Indian precedents; approved reports / primary texts for international or historic comparative decisions. Use [[Template - Case Note]] when adding a case.
