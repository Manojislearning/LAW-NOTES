# Donoghue v Stevenson [1932] AC 562

## Material facts and issue

A consumer alleged illness after consuming ginger beer in an opaque bottle said to contain a decomposed snail. The issue before the House of Lords was whether the manufacturer could owe a duty of care to the ultimate consumer even without a contract between them. At this stage the Lords assessed whether the pleaded facts disclosed a cause of action, not a final factual finding that the manufacturer was negligent.

## Ratio / significance

The decision recognised a potential duty of care to persons closely and directly affected by one's acts where injury was reasonably foreseeable. Lord Atkin's **neighbour principle** became a foundational way of explaining duty in negligence.
