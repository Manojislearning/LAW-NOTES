# Revision Plan

**Suggested spaced review (custom routine, not a university requirement):**

- First learning: draft full answer and one-page issue map.
- +1 day: close notes and recall headings, provision, two key cases.
- +3 days: rewrite short answer from memory.
- +7 days: timed answer to a past question.
- +14 days: compare similar doctrines and identify exceptions.
- +30 days: full timed practice and error correction.

Record review dates and whether you can retrieve the law **without opening the note**.

## Weekly rhythm

- Three days: learn and verify new topics.
- Two days: old-paper answers and correction.
- One day: judgment recall, bare acts and comparison tables.
- One day: consolidate weak points and plan the next week.
